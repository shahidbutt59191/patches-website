Backings & Borders Guide

Choose the ideal backing mechanism and border finish engineered for your garment and field requirements.

Option A: Merrowed Stitched Border

Classic 3D Rolled Overlock Edge

A thick 1/8" thread piping wrapped around the patch edge. This is the traditional look seen on military name tags, Boy Scout patches, and NASA badges. Ideal for standard shapes like circles, shields, and rectangles.

✓ Free of charge on all standard shapes
Option B: Laser-Cut (Heat Cut) Edge

Sharp Precision Die-Cut Edge

Industrial CNC laser cut that heat-seals the synthetic fabric border to prevent fraying. Essential for intricate custom silhouettes, wings, skulls, flames, and tactical hook & loop patches that lay perfectly flush on gear.

✓ Perfect for complex outlines & custom die cuts
